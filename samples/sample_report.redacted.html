Reading these values. Status strings are quoted exactly as the API returns them, because that is what support and bug reports need. The observed ladder is PENDING_FULFILLMENT → READY_FOR_FULFILLMENT for the order and FACTORY_OK → FACTORY_GATED → STAGED_FOR_PDI → PDI_COMPLETED → VEHICLE_PREPPED for the vehicle; a value can skip steps, and the published state lags the physical car. actualGeneralAssemblyDate is the real build; the planned and expected dates can legitimately fall later.

What this page cannot show. Before Rivian provisions the car on your account, currentUser.vehicles is empty and the vehicle-state surface answers with an all-null skeleton, so no live telemetry, location or charge level exists to read. No field anywhere names where the car physically is — the PDI/prep statuses are evidence, not a location. And status timestamps are record-level: two steps 22 minutes apart measure a database write, not the labour behind it.
